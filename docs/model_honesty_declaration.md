# JALDRISHTI AI — Model Honesty & Implementation Integrity Declaration

**Document Version:** 1.0 (Phase 9)  
**Standard:** Strict Transparency in AI/ML Reporting for Decision-Support Systems  

---

## 1. Executive Declaration

JALDRISHTI AI is designed as a disaster early-warning decision-support system. In life-critical contexts, **scientific honesty and implementation transparency take absolute precedence over marketing aesthetics**.

This document explicitly classifies every forecasting and surrogate model in the repository, stating what the model claims to do, how it is actually implemented in source code, its current lifecycle status, and the source of its evaluation data.

## 3. Data Authenticity Declaration

1. **Training Datasets:** All ML models in the `model_registry/` are currently trained on causally coupled synthetic data generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_training_data.py`.
2. **Historical Observations:** Genuine historical IMD gridded rainfall (0.25°) and NASA GPM IMERG (V07B) records are stored in `data/historical/` and `data/raw/rainfall/` for hindcast verification.
3. **SAR Flood Reference:** Historical Sentinel-1 event metadata and aggregate extent references are cataloged in `data/manifests/flood_events.yaml`.
4. **Target Development Plan (Phase 10+):** Full end-to-end retraining on genuine FABDEM tiles, computed HAND rasters, and pixel-level Sentinel-1 SAR water masks.
