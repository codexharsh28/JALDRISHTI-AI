# JALDRISHTI AI — 30-Second Differentiation Elevator Pitch

> *"JALDRISHTI AI is an integrated heavy rainfall early warning and decision-support platform that connects the entire disaster chain: deep learning nowcasting, channel routing, 2D hydraulic flood inundation, causal risk attribution, and location-aware citizen notifications.*
> 
> *Piloted for the flood-prone Mahanadi Delta in Odisha and built on a configuration-driven architecture, JALDRISHTI solves the critical last-mile problem with transparent AI explainability, strict geofencing, and military-grade cybersecurity."*

---

## Key Validated Differentiators
1. **End-to-End Causal Chaining**: Bridges precipitation nowcasting to last-mile citizen alerting without human translation lag.
2. **Transparent Causal Attribution**: Every alert explains exactly *why* risk changed ($w_r \cdot \text{Rain} + w_h \cdot \text{River} + w_i \cdot \text{Flood}$) rather than outputting an opaque score.
3. **Fail-Safe Sensor Fallbacks**: Automatic graceful degradation to GloFAS and regional NWP models with dynamic uncertainty expansion if radar/gauge telemetry drops.
4. **Zero-Fatigue Hyperlocal Geofencing**: Points-in-polygon spatial targeting alerts only citizens inside danger zones, eliminating regional warning panic.
5. **Hardened Production Security**: Standardized PostgreSQL/PostGIS spatial schemas, token-based IDOR authorization, sliding-window rate limiters, and salted zero-plaintext OTP storage.
